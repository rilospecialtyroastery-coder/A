# RSTR Coffee - Page Topology

## Site: rstrcoffee.com
## Platform: Shopify (custom theme)
## Visual Style: Minimal, black & white, bold typography, personality-driven

## Sections (top to bottom)

1. **Header/Nav** - Sticky. Logo (RSTR Coffee Trading), Log in, Search, Cart
2. **Hero Banner** - Full-width image + headline "You don't need to get your life together. Just your coffee order."
3. **In Stock Section** - Scrolling carousel of in-stock products with "in stock!" heading
4. **Best Sellers Section** - "OUR BEST SELLERS" heading, subtitle "We're not here to eat spiders!", carousel of featured products with longer descriptions
5. **Out of Stock Section** - "OUT OF STOCK" heading with "SOLD OUT. IT'S OKAY..." message, grid of sold out products
6. **Brand Partners Section** - "WE GOT THEM" section with partner logos
7. **Footer** - Social links (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Email), nav links, newsletter, payment methods, region selector

## Color Palette
- Background: #ffffff (white)
- Text: #000000 (black)  
- Secondary text: #666 (gray)
- Accent/border: #e5e5e5 (light gray)
- Sale badge: red

## Typography
- Font: likely Helvetica/Arial or a clean sans-serif
- Headings: bold, uppercase for section titles
- Body: regular weight

## Layout
- Max width: ~1400px centered
- Mobile-first responsive
- Product cards: image + title + price + button
